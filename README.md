### 🔎 How to analyze an SIH problem statement

Before choosing a problem, identify:

* What is the actual problem?
* Who experiences it?
* What is the current solution?
* Why is the current solution insufficient?
* What makes your proposed solution different?
* Is the solution technically feasible?
* Can you build a working prototype?
* How will you measure success?
* Can the solution scale beyond the hackathon?

# 🎨 SIH PPT & Presentation

Your solution can be technically brilliant—but if you cannot communicate it clearly, judges may not understand its value.

Use the recommended structure:

```text
01 — Title & Team
02 — Problem & Motivation
03 — Proposed Solution
04 — Innovation & USP
05 — Technology & Architecture
06 — Impact, Feasibility & Future Scope
```

Resources:

* [6-Slide PPT Structure](ppt/6-slide-structure.md)
* [PPT Checklist](ppt/ppt-checklist.md)
* [Presentation Guide](ppt/sample-ppt.md)

> ⚠️ Always verify the official SIH rules and presentation requirements for your specific edition before submission.

# ✅ Final SIH Checklist

Before submission, ask:

### Problem

* [ ] Do we clearly understand the problem?
* [ ] Did we identify the target users?
* [ ] Did we research existing solutions?

### Innovation

* [ ] What makes our solution different?
* [ ] Is the innovation clearly explained?
* [ ] Can we demonstrate it?

### Technology

* [ ] Is our architecture clear?
* [ ] Is our technology stack justified?
* [ ] Does our prototype actually work?

### Impact

* [ ] Who benefits?
* [ ] What measurable impact can we create?
* [ ] Can the solution scale?

### Presentation

* [ ] Is the PPT concise?
* [ ] Is the problem clearly explained?
* [ ] Is the USP obvious?
* [ ] Can every team member explain the project?
