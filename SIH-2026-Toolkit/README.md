# 🚀 SIH 2026 Toolkit

> Everything you need to go from **Problem Statement → Idea → Prototype → PPT → Submission**.

A student-friendly, open-source collection of frameworks, prompts, checklists, templates, development resources, and practical guidance for Smart India Hackathon participants.

## ✨ What's inside

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| 📋 Problem Statements | Analysis frameworks and organization |
| 💡 Idea Building | Problem understanding, validation, novelty |
| 🤖 AI Toolkit | Curated prompts for research, ideation & development |
| 🎨 PPT | 6-slide planning and pitch guidance |
| 💻 Development | GitHub, stack, APIs, deployment |
| 🧰 Resources | Datasets, APIs, AI tools, hosting |
| 📝 Templates | README, architecture, team & submission templates |

## 🗺️ The SIH Roadmap

```text
Problem Statement
       ↓
Understand the Problem
       ↓
Identify Users & Pain Points
       ↓
Research Existing Solutions
       ↓
Find the Innovation Gap
       ↓
Design Your Solution
       ↓
Build an MVP
       ↓
Test + Validate
       ↓
Prepare PPT + Demo
       ↓
Practice Judge Questions
       ↓
Submit 🚀
```
