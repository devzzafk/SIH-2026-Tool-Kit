# 🎨 6-Slide Pitch Structure

Always check the **current official SIH instructions/template** before submitting.

## Slide 1 — Title
- Team name
- Problem statement ID/title
- One-line solution

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Who faces the problem?
- What is the pain?
- Why does it matter?
- Evidence/data if available

## Slide 3 — Solution
- Product overview
- Core workflow
- Key features

## Slide 4 — Innovation + Technology
- What is different?
- Architecture
- AI/ML or other technology and why it is needed

## Slide 5 — Feasibility + Impact
- Implementation plan
- Users/stakeholders
- Expected impact
- Scalability
- Risks and mitigation

## Slide 6 — Demo / Roadmap
- MVP status
- Prototype screenshots/demo flow
- Roadmap
- Team roles / closing statement

### Golden rule
One slide = one message. Use diagrams and screenshots instead of paragraphs.
