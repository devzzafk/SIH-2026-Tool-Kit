## 4. Root cause
Ask **why?** repeatedly until you reach a process, information, access, infrastructure, behavior, or technical root cause.

## 6. Innovation gap
A strong SIH idea should answer:
- What already exists?
- Why isn't it enough?
- What are we changing?
- Why is our approach technically feasible?
- Can we demonstrate the difference?

## 7. Validation checklist
- [ ] Talked to or researched real users
- [ ] Verified the problem exists
- [ ] Studied existing solutions
- [ ] Identified measurable outcomes
